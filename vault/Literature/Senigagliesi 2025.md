# Senigagliesi 2025

Hub: [[00 - Project Context]] · Index: [[Literature Index]] · Context: [[Prior Art Verdict]]

Citation + URL: L. Senigagliesi, G. Ciattaglia, E. Gambi, "Autoencoder-based physical layer authentication in a real indoor environment," Phys. Commun., 2025, doi 10.1016/j.phycom.2025.102626 (DOI from the proposal reference list; not resolved this session).

What it does (UNVERIFIED, abstract only): sparse autoencoder trained only on the legitimate user; detects forged signals; reported geometric-mean accuracy above 90% on real USRP data.

Overlap with our work: shows one-class autoencoder physical-layer authentication is established on the wireless side. Wireless only, no fiber, attacker apparently not adaptive.

Evidence labels: UNVERIFIED.

Implications / links: weakens any novelty claim for the autoencoder alone ([[Claims and Risks]]).
